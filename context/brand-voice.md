# LinkedIn Outreach Voice

## Positioning

Full-stack developer and software engineer helping U.S. businesses, founders, SaaS teams and agencies build websites, web applications, dashboards, integrations and AI automation.

## Outreach goal

Start a real business conversation with a qualified buyer. The first touch should feel like a researched peer conversation, not a sales sequence. Move toward a discovery conversation only when there is clear fit.

## Default style

Question-led, direct, calm and human. Use the prospect's actual situation, current project, post, launch, technical gap or stated priority.

Keep first-touch messages short. One clear idea. One question at most. No generic flattery.

## Personalization standard

Every qualified prospect should have:

- A specific buying signal.
- A source URL that was actually inspected.
- A dated signal.
- A concrete reason the service fits.
- A relevant proof point from the user's portfolio or experience.
- A clear outreach angle.
- A verified LinkedIn contact path.

Useful proof examples include Recover Revenue, Next Role, Marlin Hotel, ForYouHub Shopify operations, Revenue OS, AI voice-agent workflows, custom dashboards, SaaS products and full-stack client applications.

## Avoid

Do not use:

- "I came across your profile."
- "I wanted to reach out."
- Empty compliments.
- Generic service lists.
- Fabricated claims about the prospect.
- Fabricated metrics or results.
- A pitch before understanding the need.
- Multiple questions in one message.
- Pressure after a negative response.
- Automated or bulk LinkedIn actions.

## Follow-ups

Default LinkedIn sequence:

1. Connection or first DM.
2. One useful follow-up after approximately two business days when there is no reply.
3. Stop or move to nurture when the signal is stale or the prospect declines.

The system can store additional sequence steps, but the active LinkedIn default is bounded rather than an indefinite chase.

## Reply handling

Classify the actual reply first, then prepare a response. Preserve the prospect's wording in the record. Response drafts stay editable until the user approves and manually sends them.

## Language and formatting

English by default. Match the prospect's language when clear.

No em-dash punctuation in outreach. Keep sentences short. Keep the first touch under the configured character limit.

## Approval boundary

Research, scoring, organization, drafting, reminders and reporting may be automated.

Connection requests, messages, follows, likes, comments and publishing remain manual and require explicit user approval.
